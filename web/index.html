stardew3ds converter

Turns your own copy of Stardew Valley into the files the 3DS game needs.

Nothing is uploaded. This page reads your game files on your own computer and builds the 3DS files right here in the tab. Your game, mods and save never leave it. See the code.

1 Your Stardew Valley folder

In Steam: right-click Stardew Valley, then Manage, then Browse local files. Pick that folder (or just its Content folder).

not picked

StardewValley.GameData.dll

The converter reads the game's data files with this one file from your game. It sits in the Stardew Valley folder, right next to Content. It wasn't in the folder you picked, so add it here.

not picked

2 Mods optional

Your Mods folder with Stardew Valley Expanded, Ridgeside Village or other Content Patcher packs. A Mods folder inside the game folder is found on its own.

none

3 Your PC farm optional

To carry on a farm from your PC, pick your Saves folder (or one farm's folder inside it, named like Farmer_123456789). Saves aren't kept in the game folder:

  • Windows: %AppData%\StardewValley\Saves (paste that into the folder window's address bar)
  • Linux and Mac: ~/.config/StardewValley/Saves
none

Getting ready...

4 Put it on your SD card

Unzip it onto the top level of your 3DS SD card, so its 3ds folder joins the one already there. The game itself (stardew3ds.3dsx) is included: open it from the Homebrew Launcher. Your save and backups on the card are left alone.
