# Raiden (ItsRaiden)

The porter's own character: he sits in the Saloon with his 3DS and tells you a Nintendo fact
when you say hi. Pixel art from ItsRaiden's reference sheets, at the game's scale:

- `Portraits_Raiden.png`: 64x64 portraits (0/2: playing, eyes on the DS; 1/3: looking at you)
- `Characters_Raiden.png`: 16x32 frames, rows down/right/up/left, then sitting with the DS

The app ships them as `romfs/tex/*_Raiden.s3t` (rebuild with `convert_texture` in tools/convert.py).
